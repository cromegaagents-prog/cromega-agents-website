§ 01What's includedSpec sheet W

Every buildincludes

Scope changes by business. These are the parts every Cromega website is built from.

  1. W-01

    Custom design & development

    A layout, type and visual direction made for your business, built and hosted by us. Nothing is a reskinned template.

  2. W-02

    Branding

    We work from your logo and colors, or develop a refined direction if you're starting fresh or it's time for a change.

  3. W-03

    Mobile layouts

    Most visitors arrive on a phone. We design that screen with as much care as the desktop, not as a squeezed copy of it.

  4. W-04

    Service pages

    Clear pages for what you offer, who it's for and how to start, written around the questions customers ask before they buy.

  5. W-05

    Calls to action

    One obvious next step on every page: call, book, request a quote or visit. Visitors shouldn't have to hunt for it.

  6. W-06

    Inquiry forms & booking

    Forms that send inquiries to you, plus booking integrations where your booking tool supports it.

  7. W-07

    Basic on-page SEO

    Page titles, descriptions, headings, image text and business details set up so search engines can read the site.

§ 03Launch processSix steps

From first callto live site.

You see the design before anything is built and approve the site before it goes live. We set the schedule after discovery, once the scope is clear.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Services, customers, competitors and goals. We review your current site if you have one.

  2. 02

    Plan & content

    Pages, calls to action and content list. We gather photos, logo and service details.

  3. 03

    Design

    You review a custom design for desktop and mobile, and we refine it together.

  4. 04

    Build

    Development, forms and booking where supported, plus on-page SEO.

  5. 05

    Review & launch

    You check it on your own phone and computer. After approval, we launch on your domain.

  6. 06

    Care

    Hosting, updates and support so the site stays fast and current.

        § 08Questions

        Good toknow.

        How long does a website take?

        It depends on the number of pages, the features and how quickly content comes together. You get a schedule with checkpoints after the discovery call.

        Can you redesign the site I already have?

        Yes. We keep what's working, like your name, reputation and any content worth saving, and rebuild the design and structure.

        Do you write the words and take the photos?

        We shape the copy with you around what customers ask and advise on photos. You supply the business details and approve everything before launch.

        Can customers book online?

        Yes, where your booking tool allows it. We connect supported booking systems or link to them clearly from every page.

        What does maintenance cover?

        Hosting, updates, security and support. Higher plans add automation and more hands-on help.

        Will the site rank on Google?

        We set up basic on-page SEO so search engines can read the site. Rankings depend on many factors outside the website, so we don't promise positions.

        Ready for a sitethat works?

        Send a few lines about the business. We'll reply with questions, not a sales script.

        § 01Our approachSix disciplines

        Careful work,not volume.

        1. 01

          Target customer definition

          We describe your best customers with you: type of business or person, size, needs, and the signals that someone is ready to buy.

        2. 02

          Prospect research

          Our team finds businesses or people who match that profile and gathers what's needed to approach them well.

        3. 03

          Qualification

          Each prospect is checked against the criteria you approved, so your time goes to the ones worth talking to.

        4. 04

          Pipeline organization

          Every prospect has a stage, notes and a next step, so nothing is lost between first contact and handoff.

        5. 05

          Follow-up

          Personal follow-up from our team at a respectful pace. It is not automated today.

        6. 06

          Reporting

          Plain-language updates on who was researched, contacted and qualified, and what comes next.

        § 02The journeyProspect → handoff

        From a nameto a handoff.

        The intended path for every prospect. The last step is yours: you take the conversation with everything we learned.

        1. Find

          A prospect matches your target profile.

        2. Research

          We learn about their business and likely needs.

        3. Qualify

          They're checked against your criteria.

        4. Reach out

          Our team makes contact and follows up personally.

        5. Conversation

          The prospect agrees to a sales conversation.

        6. Handoff

          You get the prospect, our notes and the context.

        § 06Questions

        Straightanswers.

        Do you guarantee a number of leads?

        No. Results depend on your market, your offer and how qualified a lead needs to be. We agree on goals together and report honestly on what happens.

        How much does it cost?

        Lead generation is scoped to your market and goals, so pricing is confirmed after the first conversation.

        How soon will I get conversations?

        That depends on your market and criteria. You'll see what we're finding from the first prospect list onward.

        Who talks to the prospects?

        Our team handles research, outreach and follow-up. When a prospect is ready for a sales conversation, we hand them to you.

        Is outreach or booking automated?

        Not today. Follow-up is personal. Automated outreach and booking are planned for Cromega OS and aren't operational.

        Do I need a website from you?

        No, but a strong site helps prospects trust you. If yours is holding you back, we'll say so.

        Let's find yournext customers.

        Tell us who you sell to and who you wish you sold to.
