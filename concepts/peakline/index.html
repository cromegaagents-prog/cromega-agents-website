Stage 1 of 5Framing & deck

1Framing & deck

Roof replacement & structural repair

We start from a sound structure. Damaged rafters and decking are repaired or replaced before anything goes on top, so the new roof sits flat and stays fastened.

  • Tear-off and disposal of the old roof
  • Rafter and ridge repairs where needed
  • New roof decking, fastened to spec

2Underlayment

Leak protection

A synthetic underlayment rolls out course by course from the eaves up, overlapped so water always runs over a seam, never into one.

  • Synthetic underlayment across the whole deck
  • Ice and water barrier at eaves and valleys
  • Overlaps set so water sheds downhill

3Standing-seam panels

Standing-seam metal roofs

Continuous steel panels run from eave to ridge with raised seams and concealed fasteners, so there are no exposed screws to work loose.

  • Panels measured and cut for your roof
  • Concealed fasteners under raised seams
  • Six colors, matte or satin finish

4Ridge, flashing & gutters

Flashing, trim & gutters

The details keep water out: a ridge cap along the peak, flashing around the chimney, drip edge and rake trim, then gutters and downspouts matched to the roof.

  • Ridge cap and ventilation
  • Chimney and wall flashing
  • Seamless gutters and downspouts
